About

Bossfall is a fast paced Sci-Fi Action RPG Boss Rush. Fight a gauntlet of the universe's most lethal warriors and monsters to rise as the arena's ultimate champion.

Master tight controls to shoot and dash your way through dynamic combat. Every victory rewards you with legendary loot, allowing you to endlessly customize your playstyle and adapt to new challenges.

Features

  • Action-Packed Arena Combat: Face off against a diverse roster of powerful monsters and formidable adversaries.
  • Dedicated Boss Rush: Test both your mechanical skills and your build crafting strategy in an exciting mode against back to back boss fights.
  • Deep Customization: Mix and match legendary weapons, spells, and gear to create game breaking builds.
  • Tight Mechanics: Master responsive dash and shoot gameplay to effortlessly outmaneuver incoming attacks.
  • High Replayability: Experiment with different loadouts to conquer higher difficulties for even better rewards.
